About Cx2 Homebuyers

Cx2 Homebuyers is a local, owner-run company in Houston, Texas that makes fair, as-is cash offers on houses within about an hour of central Houston. We sign a purchase contract with the homeowner and often assign it to one of the local cash investors we work with, who closes at a licensed title company. Sellers pay no commissions, repairs or closing costs, and we're only paid when a sale closes.

How we work

  1. We talk. We reach out because we buy houses in your neighborhood, or you call or text us. We'll ask a little about the house and what you need.
  2. Cooper walks through the house. He does every walk-through himself, at a time that works for you. There's nothing to clean or fix first.
  3. You get a written offer. Usually within a day or two, with the math behind it. There's no obligation, and you can take all the time you need.
  4. You pick the closing date. If you say yes, a licensed Texas title company handles the paperwork and pays you directly at closing.

How we come up with our offers

We start with what similar houses nearby have recently sold for, then account for the repairs your house needs and the costs to buy, fix and resell it. Our offers are usually below full market value, because you're trading some of the price for speed, certainty and selling as-is. If listing with an agent would likely get you more, we'll tell you.

How we're paid

We don't charge you commissions or fees. We're paid a fee for arranging the sale, and only when it actually closes. In many cases we assign your purchase contract to one of the local cash investors we work with, and they pay our fee at closing. Texas law requires us to tell you this in writing before you sign (Texas Occupations Code §1101.0045), and we always do.
