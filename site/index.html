Frequently asked questions

Is Cx2 Homebuyers legit?

Yes. Cx2 Homebuyers LLC is a registered Texas company based in Houston, and every sale closes at a licensed Texas title company that holds the money and handles the paperwork. We never ask you for upfront fees, deposits or bank details. You're welcome to have a family member, attorney or agent review our offer before you sign.

How do you determine your offer?

We look at what similar houses nearby have recently sold for, then account for the repairs your house needs and the costs of buying, fixing and reselling it. We'll walk you through the numbers when we give you the offer.

Are there any fees or commissions?

No. You don't pay any commissions or fees, and the buyer pays the normal closing costs.

How fast can you close?

It depends mostly on the title company clearing title. In some cases we can close in as little as 2 days once title is clear. If you need more time to move, you can pick a later date.

Do I need to make repairs or clean the house?

No. We buy houses as-is. Take what you want and leave the rest.

Can I sell if I'm behind on payments or have liens?

Often, yes. The title company pays off your mortgage and any liens from the sale proceeds. The sooner you reach out, the more options you'll have.

Who actually buys my house?

We sign a purchase contract with you, and in many cases we assign that contract to one of the local cash investors we work with, who closes at the title company. We're paid a fee for arranging the sale. Texas law requires us to tell you this in writing before you sign, and we'll walk you through it.

Are you real estate agents?

No. We're not licensed agents and we don't represent you. You're always free to have your own agent or attorney review anything we send.

Am I obligated to accept your offer?

Not at all. Our offers are free and come with no obligation. If it doesn't work for you, that's completely fine.
